The pig, the snake and the pigeon by Ethan Yuan is a 2/10 movie

This movie was really popular in china a while back and today we watched it in class. We watched a pirated version with many cuts and the guy playing it kept fast-forwarding silent scenes. But did that change my opinions on the movie? Hell nah! This is a hollow and illogical popcorn movie with tons of meaningless violence, but manages to be self-absorbingly pretentious at the same time. The portrayal of some characters was downright disgusting and misogynistic. I wouldnt have beared that shit if i didnt want to write a review. The only kinda saving grace was the cult scene and that wasnt very good either
