OPAL by Jack Stauber is a 9/10 video

This was one of the best 10 minutes i've ever spent, and definitely my favourite animated short! Everything about the aesthetic style of the video is so unique and otherworldly and conveys a sense of dread, and the breathless music with its heavy use of the main motif does a great service to the story. And of course the plot twist is heartbreaking. I watched this every day for a week and found something new every watch. I have some criticisms though, like the dad segment doesnt focus much on opal and the mom segment seemed too intentionally creepy.
