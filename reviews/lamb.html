Lamb to the slaughter and other short stories by Roald Dahl is a 6.5/10 book

I mean it was alright i guess. The plots were simple and not particularly interesting. I read most of them expecting a big twist or payoff at the end but it stays uneventful. A lot of plot points were pretty old-fashioned too. I definitely like his children's books far more. The descriptions and characterisations were pretty good, though. My favourite story was the one titled 'skin'.
