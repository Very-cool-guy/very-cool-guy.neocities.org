Deltarune double meanings theory

this theory posits that some mysteries in deltarune have two simultaneous and seemingly conflicting answers. ok its more of a headcanon cuz its more vibes based

firstly, the angel's identity is noelle in the weird route and the player in the normal route. The amount of evidence supporting both candidates are astounding and i was torn. Then i replayed undertale and noticed the legend about the angel, who would "[make] the underground be empty", has drastically differing interpretations depending on the route. this seems like obvious foreshadowing. i believe these characters being the final boss of their respective routes is a logical and satisfying way to ultimately fulfill the prophecy.

however, i mainly wanted to talk about this next idea i really like. dess had two deaths: a fantastical death involving FRIEND and the bunker and whatnot; and a realistic death where she drowned herself. if youve seen the life of pi its exactly like that: neither of deaths will be implied as "truer" than the other. firstly dess's dissappearance obviously had something to do with the magic stuff. for example, the children followed the pointed tail. but chapter 5 added a lot to the dess drowned theory. in noelle's conversation with susie by the lake, she confesses her thoughts of suicide with the euphemism of "leaving". curiously in the mirroring chapter 5 monologue, she uses the exact same phrasing to describe dess's dissappearance. furthermore, we now know that going under the lake would bring you out of bounds, which can explain why dess is lost in the code. dark fountains are frequently described as water, which can hint that kris is making it up to suppress their trauma about dess. this theory being true will also be consistent with toby saying a game that recently came out is similar to deltarune, i.e. omori. i just think this would be very poignant cuz themes and such
